GEMFIRE

One tab runs the game. Friends join with a code and play as players 2, 3 and 4.

How to play together

  1. Host. Press Host a game. You get a 6-character code. Send the code, or Copy link. You are player 1. Leave this tab open and in front — it is the console.
  2. Join. Your friend unlocks the same site, presses Join, and types the code. They see your screen and use their own controller.
  3. Turns. Gemfire is turn-based, so a short delay on a friend's screen is fine. Their buttons come back here as controller ports 2–4. Up to three friends can join.

Play on this tab

Save states and fullscreen are on the emulator bar. Saves stay in this browser.

GEMFIRE

Arrows or WASD move. Z A, X B, C C, Enter or Space is Start. A gamepad works too.

How to play

Host. You are player 1. Arrows move. Z is A, X is B, C is C, Enter is Start. A gamepad works. The emulator bar has save states and fullscreen. Keep the tab open and visible.

Join. You watch the host's screen. Same keys, and WASD also moves. The buttons under the picture work on a phone. You are player 2, 3 or 4, on your own controller port.

Multitap. Three and four players need an adapter. This page starts with the EA 4-Way Play, which maps players 1–4 to ports 1–4. If Gemfire never offers a 4-player game, switch the menu to Sega Team Player. That restarts the ROM, because the game checks the adapter at power-on.

Limits. The host's browser is the game. Friends get the picture and sound from that tab, so there is a little delay, and the game cannot drift out of sync. A computer is the best host. Some strict networks block the connection. Matchmaking uses the public PeerJS service, with a public relay as a fallback.
